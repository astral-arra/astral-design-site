Often Asked Questions

What is Astral Design?

Astral Design is a home for Human Design. It brings together Astral Hub — software that calculates and explains your chart — personal reports written for your exact chart, and an AI Library that answers your questions with sources. Your free chart is the starting point for all of it.

How is Human Design connected to Astral Design?

Human Design is the system; Astral Design is how we help you use it. Everything we make — the bodygraph in Astral Hub, the transits, the reports and the AI answers — is calculated from your birth data according to Human Design and explained in plain language, so you can apply it in your daily life.

What is Human Design?

A system that brings together astrology, the I Ching, the Kabbalah and the chakra system in one chart, calculated from your birth data. It is a tool for self-reflection.

Why do I need my exact birth time?

Several parts of the chart change within minutes. If you only know an approximate time, we show which parts of your chart may differ.

Is the free chart really free?

Yes. Your bodygraph, Type, Strategy and Authority are free forever. Paid plans add transits, connection charts, the AI Library and more.

Can I cancel anytime?

What we believe

Three principles behind everything we make

Precision first

Every chart is calculated from your exact birth time and place, with time zones and daylight saving handled for you. If your time is uncertain, we tell you which parts of the chart may change.

Clear language

Human Design has its own vocabulary. We explain every term the first time you meet it, so the chart becomes something you can live with — not decode.

Your data stays yours

Your birth data is used to calculate your chart and answer your questions — nothing else. You can edit or delete it from your account at any time.
