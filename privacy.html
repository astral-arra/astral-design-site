Google Analytics

If you click “Accept” in the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and collects aggregated information: which pages you visit, roughly where you are (country/city), your device and browser, and how you found the site. We keep this data for 14 months and do not use it for advertising. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.
